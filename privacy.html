Capture Workstation Privacy Policy

Effective: August 29, 2026

Capture Workstation is developed and published by Faison Digital Works LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how Capture Workstation handles information when you use the application.

Information Collection

Capture Workstation does not require a user account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with Faison Digital Works LLC.

Screen Captures and Images

Capture Workstation allows users to capture screen content and open, edit, annotate, save, and export image files. These actions are initiated by the user.

Screen captures, images, annotations, and exported files remain under the user's control and are not automatically transmitted to Faison Digital Works LLC.

Local File Access

The application may access files and folders selected by the user for the purpose of opening, editing, saving, or exporting images. Capture Workstation does not independently scan or upload unrelated files from the user's device.

Internet and Background Activity

Capture Workstation does not use its desktop permissions to collect personal information, run undisclosed background services, or perform actions without user initiation.

Advertising and Analytics

Capture Workstation does not contain third-party advertising and does not use third-party behavioral tracking or advertising analytics.

Purchases

Purchases and licensing made through the Microsoft Store are processed by Microsoft under Microsoft's own terms and privacy practices. Faison Digital Works LLC does not receive customers' payment-card information.

Children's Privacy

Capture Workstation does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Capture Workstation's features or information-handling practices change. The current version will be published on this page.
