Capture Workstation Privacy Policy

Effective: August 29, 2026

Capture Workstation is developed and published by Faison Digital Works, LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how Capture Workstation handles information when you use the application.

Information Collection

Capture Workstation does not require a user account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with Faison Digital Works, LLC.

Screen Captures and Images

The application enables users to capture screen content and perform editing, annotation, saving, and export functions. Screen captures, images, annotations, and exported files remain under the user's control and are not automatically transmitted to Faison Digital Works, LLC.

Local File Access

The application may access files and folders selected by the user for the purpose of opening, editing, saving, or exporting images. It does not independently scan or upload unrelated files from your device.

Internet and Background Activity

Capture Workstation does not use its desktop permissions to collect personal information, run undisclosed background services, or perform actions without user initiation.

Advertising and Analytics

The application contains no third-party advertising and does not employ behavioral tracking or advertising analytics tools.

Purchases

Microsoft Store transactions are handled by Microsoft under its own terms. Faison Digital Works, LLC does not receive payment card information.

Children's Privacy

Capture Workstation does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the application's features or practices change. The current version is always published on this page.
